What it does

Three tools, one focused app — no clutter, no upsells.

+−

Add & Subtract

Pick a date, type a number, choose days, weeks, months or years. Toggle business-days mode to skip weekends and public holidays automatically.

↔

Duration Between

Tap two dates. See the breakdown in years, months and days, plus totals in business days, weeks and hours.

⏱

Countdowns

Track visa expirations, court dates, exams, anniversaries. Optional reminders fire locally. One tap adds events to your iOS Calendar.

Private by design

No accounts. No servers. No analytics. No ads.

⛔

Zero network calls

Holiday data for 150+ countries is bundled at build time. The app makes no network requests in normal use.

🔒

Your data stays local

Countdowns, calculations and preferences live in the iOS app sandbox on your device — nowhere else.

↩︎

Export or delete anytime

Share a JSON copy of your data through the iOS share sheet, or wipe everything from this device with one tap.
